# Source verification and rights gate

The bundled [source inventory](../fixtures/source_catalog.json) contains 68 distinct publishers checked on 2026-10-08/09 UTC: 21 national, 43 regional/local, and 4 international. It includes leads for every Greek region and several smaller communities. Every candidate imports **disabled** with discovery, fetch, storage, AI and reuse permission flags off. The `target_region` and `locality_hint` fields are research leads kept in the audit trail, not official administrative IDs or article place evidence. URL availability or a public RSS link is not a commercial reuse or model-processing license. This is a discovery log, not legal authorization or proof of regional reporting coverage.

The 48 RSS leads have parseable feeds, allowed feed URLs under `robots.txt`, and sampled same-host article links at the time checked. They include Ta Nea, Skai, CNN Greece, Proto Thema, ERTNews and Naftemporiki (national), France24 English (world), and regional publishers such as Voria, Kozan.gr, Evros News, EpirusPost, Larissanet, Corfu Press, Pelop, LamiaReport, ArcadiaPortal, Samos Today, Syros Today and Haniotika Nea. This is a point-in-time technical check, **not** permission to retain, process, summarize or republish feed content, nor a guarantee that a feed carries stories from every locality in its region.

The remaining 20 are **homepage-only leads**: Notia (Attica), TyposThes (Central Macedonia), Xronos (Eastern Macedonia and Thrace), Enimerosi (Ionian Islands), TheBest (Western Greece), Evima (Central Greece), Sto Nisi, Politis Chios and Empros Lesvos (North Aegean), Rodiaki and Parianos Typos (South Aegean), CretaLive (Crete), Kathimerini, In.gr, News247, Dnews and LiFO (national), and BBC, Deutsche Welle and WHO (world). No ingestable feed and supported adapter were verified for these entries. Xronos and Parianos Typos publish restrictive reuse terms; TheBest's advertised `/feed` did not return a usable feed. BBC's RSS redirects to a different host from its item links, which the current same-host ingestion rejects. Do not treat a homepage check or a guessed feed URL as a working feed.

Before allowing verification, an operator must recheck the actual feed URL, HTTP responses, `robots.txt`, canonical host, timestamps/IDs, source ownership, and relevant terms (including third-party materials). Obtain and record explicit permission for each action to be enabled: discovery, fetch, storage, AI processing, text reuse and image reuse; add a dated grant/review, expiration where applicable, supported extraction strategy, organization/syndication group, poll interval and verification note. The worker then discovers eligible RSS/Atom sources and activates them after endpoint checks; disabled or undocumented sources are never polled. Do not set permissions by assuming official or public means unrestricted. Follow takedown and expiry requests, and turn sources off when rights change. The `feed_metadata` strategy does not extract fact claims; a live pilot still needs permitted source-specific evidence extraction/curation and a vetted complete geography register before nationwide or small-community articles can be reliably generated.
